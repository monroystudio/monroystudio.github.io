2. Scope and data handled

This notice covers the informational website and communications with the operator. The site's code has no account system, forms, JavaScript, analytics, advertising, social embeds, cookies or browser storage. Fonts, styles and images are local site resources. Email links open your email application; the site itself transmits no contact form.

GitHub Pages provides hosting. GitHub states that it records visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. The operator has not verified access to individual hosting logs or control of their retention.

When you write to the official Gmail address, the operator receives your email address, sender name if supplied, subject, message, dates, correspondence metadata and any attachments you send. For privacy requests, the record may include the action requested, proportionate evidence of identity or representation, necessary correspondence references, decision, notification and completion record. Gmail may also keep associated contact suggestions and provide smart features under its settings.

Sensitive data, passwords, financial details, private videos and full identity documents are not requested for ordinary support. Do not send unnecessary information. For a privacy request requiring identity evidence, contact the official email for proportionate verification instructions before sending an unredacted document. The operator must assess unsolicited excess data and arrange lawful disposal when it is unnecessary.

The Desktop application and TikTok integration are future projects. This website does not receive TikTok credentials, profile data or videos for upload. This notice does not authorize future application processing; a notice reflecting the actual product must be provided before it starts.

6. ARCO requests by email

You may request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition, withdraw consent or ask to limit use/disclosure through monroystudio.support@gmail.com. The suggested subject “Privacy / ARCO — Monroy Studio” is optional. Spanish and English are accepted. No purchase, app account, TikTok connection or public issue is required.

Include the data subject's name, a notification channel, the requested action and information helping locate the records. Except for access, describe the relevant data. Access may concern all your data without knowing the operator's exact records. For correction, state the change and provide supporting evidence.

Identity and, when relevant, authority to represent another person must be established proportionately under applicable rules before disclosure or a protected action. For the sender’s own low-risk correspondence, control of the original channel together with a secret case receipt previously established and delivered to that channel may be used within that limited scope. A public reference number or email address alone is insufficient. A changed or unverified channel, conflicting information, sensitive data, significant consequences or representation of another person requires additional verification before disclosure or modification. The operator will explain necessary evidence and legally admissible alternatives; an official ID is not demanded routinely. Unnecessary fields may be obscured where verification remains possible. No secure upload portal or automated civil-identity or representation-verification service exists.

The decision is due within 20 business days from receipt. If granted, the action is due within 15 business days following communication of the decision. Each period may be extended once by the same length when justified and notified before it expires. The internal acknowledgment target is 2 business days and does not alter the legal clock.

If necessary, one clarification request may be made within 5 business days after receipt. You have 10 business days from the day after receiving it to respond. This interrupts the response period, which starts again the day after clarification is supplied. Without a timely answer, the request is treated as not presented; you may submit a new request.

Electronic handling and readable electronic delivery are free under the adopted procedure. A verified notification channel will be used. Any full or partial denial, absence of records, legal exception or required blocking must be explained with its grounds and scope.

If dissatisfied, you may seek protection of rights before the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, generally within 15 business days after the response is communicated. For lack of response, the procedure may be initiated once the response deadline expires. Internal follow-up does not suspend this remedy or its deadline; consult the authority's current official filing channels.
